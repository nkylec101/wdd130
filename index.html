Cebu, Philippines

Also known as the Queen City of the South in the Philippines. Cebu is a very nice place with beautiful beaches, great food, and friendly people. Speaking of great food, the Lechon baboy (whole roasted pig) is a delicasy which Cebu is famous for. People in Cebu called themselves, cebuanos, and we speak our own dialect called, bisaya (whicgh is different from the main langauge, tagalog).
